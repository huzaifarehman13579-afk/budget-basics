01 — LEARN

Budgeting Basics

A budget is a simple plan for how you will use your money.

Income

Money you receive from allowance, work, business or other sources.

Fixed Expenses

Expenses that usually stay the same, such as rent or subscriptions.

Variable Expenses

Expenses that can change each month, such as food and shopping.

Savings

Money kept aside for future needs, emergencies or goals.

06 — AVOID

Common Money Mistakes

Problem: Buying something immediately without considering whether you actually need it.

Better Action: Wait 24 hours before making unnecessary purchases.

Small daily purchases can add up. Track them and include them in your budget.

Missing payment deadlines can create unnecessary fees. Keep reminders for important payments.

Review subscriptions regularly and cancel services you no longer use.

Without a plan it is difficult to understand where your money goes. Create a simple monthly budget.
